Privacy Policy

Effective Date:

This Privacy Policy explains how NoteFlow ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using NoteFlow, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your name and email address, received from your sign-in provider (Clerk) when you create an account.
  • Your content: The notes you create, edit, and delete within the app, including any AI-generated summaries.
  • Usage data: Basic technical information such as device type and app version, used only to deliver and improve the service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To store, sync, and display your notes.
  • To generate AI summaries you request (your note content is sent to our summarization service only for this purpose).
  • To provide support and improve the app.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted service providers strictly to operate the app, including:

  • Clerk — for secure sign-in and account management.
  • PostgreSQL hosting provider — for storing your account data and notes.
  • AI summarization provider (Groq via LangChain) — solely to generate summaries you request.

Data Security

Your notes are tied to your authenticated account and are only accessible to you. We use industry-standard measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time — see our Account Deletion page for instructions.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. You may also stop using the app and remove it from your device at any time.

Children's Privacy

NoteFlow is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page, and the revised policy will apply to information collected after the effective date.
