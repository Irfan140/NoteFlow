Delete Your NoteFlow Account

How to request permanent deletion of your account and data.

What data we store

When you use NoteFlow, we store the following information tied to your account:

  • Your name and email address (provided by your sign-in provider).
  • The notes you create, their content, and any AI-generated summaries.

How to delete your account

NoteFlow does not currently offer a one-tap "Delete Account" button inside the app. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, please email us using the address associated with your account:

Email: irfanmehmud140@gmail.com

In your request, include the email address you signed up with so we can verify ownership.

What happens after you request deletion

  • We will permanently delete your account and all your notes and summaries from our servers.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone once completed.
  • We will confirm by email once the process is finished, typically within 30 days.
Note: Even after account deletion, you may also delete your sign-in identity directly through your account provider if you signed up using a third-party service.
